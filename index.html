From The Founders

James GalliganFounder, A Players Club@jamesgalligann

I started young. Sales and leadership for Iman Gadzhi, one of the biggest names in the space. Made real money before I knew what to do with it.

What I figured out fast: the people around you decide everything. How quickly you grow. What you think is possible. Whether you make it or quietly fade out at €30k a month.

The room I needed didn’t exist. Founders my age, on the same path, actually building. Most of what I saw was either too old, too American, or too busy selling courses.

So I built it.

A Players Club is now 180+ founders across Europe doing €15k to €500k a month. 25+ masterminds since 2024. We’ve also scaled that business to over €200,000 a month. The room I wish someone had handed me at twenty-one. Built for the entrepreneurs serious about the long game.

What I want you to walk away with: clarity on your next move, partnerships that compound, and the people you’ll build the next decade with.

Christian SchütteFounder, Code Light@askschuette

I’ve been building real companies for over a decade. €40M portfolio. CEO of Germany’s largest branding agency. The software processing €200M a year in F1 ticket sales. Brand systems for Mercedes, Red Bull, and most of the luxury brands you’d recognize.

Five exits along the way. I’ve made every mistake worth making twice.

These days I privately advise founders running €500k to €2M a month. Same conversation every time. The business has outgrown how it’s being run, and nobody around them has done what comes next. They’re the smartest person in their own room, and that’s the problem.

That’s why I sit at this table.

What I want you to walk away with: the bridge from founder inside the business, to CEO running it. The thing nobody around you can teach, because they’ve never crossed it themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Contact us
Who should attend the A Players Dubai chapter?

Founders serious about the long game who want to be in a room with people ahead of them, on the same path. Business owners at every scale, from the first hire to the hundredth.

What’s the schedule like?

Friday is VVIP only: a closed-door mastermind roundtable with the Mastermind speakers and a hypnosis session with Ebrahim from 2:00 PM, then the VVIP private dinner experience at SushiSamba at 9:00 PM. Saturday is the main stage from 10:00 AM, with two hours of personalised roundtable workshops at the speakers’ tables, then an evening reception. Sunday is a morning yacht, then the Desert Evening Experience for VIP and VVIP guests: dinner with live entertainment and a DJ. VVIPs bring a plus-one.

What pricing options are available?

General Admission covers Saturday. VIP covers Saturday and Sunday. VVIP adds all of Friday, the closed-door roundtable and the private dinner at SushiSamba, plus a plus-one for the Sunday Desert Evening Experience. It is limited to 12 seats and needs an application or referral. A Players members receive 20% off every ticket.

Who are the speakers?

Dan Henry, Jake Trinder, James Galligan, Marius Bulai, Christian Schütte and Ebrahim Turner, plus a special guest announced soon.

Where in Dubai is it?

Friday is in a private boardroom, with dinner at SushiSamba. The Saturday venue is announced soon. Every address goes to ticket holders first.

How is this different from other networking events?

It’s small on purpose. 50 seats and three days with the same people, so the conversations go past introductions.

Are flights and hotels included?

No. Flights and accommodation are your own. Everything on the itinerary for your ticket is included.

How do I know if I’ll fit in with the other attendees?

Everyone in the room is building something. If you’re serious about the next stage of your business, you’ll fit in. If you’re not sure, email us and we’ll tell you honestly.
