Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

A Players Dubai is the Dubai weekend of the A Players Global Tour, run by A Players from Dublin, Ireland. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it and what you can ask of us. For any question about it, write to hello@aplayerstour.com.

What we collect

  • The details you give when you reserve a ticket or request a VVIP seat: your name, email address, phone number, company, country, monthly revenue band, what you are building and the ticket you choose.
  • Anything you send us by email.
  • Information about how you found and use the site, such as the campaign that brought you here, the pages visited and the device and browser, collected through cookies and similar tools.

Why we use it

We use your details to take your reservation, to contact you about your ticket or application and to run the event. We use information about how the site is used to understand which campaigns and pages work and to improve them. Our legal bases are the contract you enter into when you buy a ticket and the steps you ask us to take before it, our legitimate interest in running and improving our events, and your consent where the law requires it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers that host this site, take payment for tickets, store and follow up on reservations and enquiries, and measure how the site and our advertising are used, and only for those purposes. Payment is taken by Whop, which handles your card details under its own terms; we never see them. Some of these providers may store data outside the European Economic Area, in which case we rely on the safeguards the law provides for such transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as the law requires.

Cookies

The site uses cookies to measure how it is used and how our advertising performs. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it or withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to hello@aplayerstour.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland at dataprotection.ie.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with the date it was updated.
